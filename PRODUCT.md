# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary: people evaluating Yixin Zhang for research opportunities and PhD admission, including prospective supervisors and admissions reviewers. Secondary: recruiters for relevant robotics and applied-AI work.

## Product Purpose

This personal portfolio helps its author, who is actively seeking PhD opportunities in robot learning, earn research conversations and admission consideration by making a credible trajectory from systems engineering and applied AI toward robot learning easy to understand and inspect. Success is that an appropriate reviewer can understand the work and its evidence without treating the site as a conventional résumé alone.

## Positioning

An evidence-led robotics and applied-AI portfolio: each technical case separates what was implemented, what an artifact directly supports, reported or modeled results, reference material, and what remains unresolved. This makes the limits of the evidence as legible as the work itself.

## Research Interests

Primary interest: robot learning, especially how deployment failures can inform better policies. Additional forward-looking interests: infrastructure for autonomous agents, including Web3 approaches; AI for mathematics; and AI for biology (for example, proteins) and medicine (for example, radiomics). These are interests, not claims of completed projects.

## Operating Context

Visitors review a static nine-slide web portfolio covering the profile and career trajectory, robot systems, industrial computer vision, climate analytics, reinforcement learning, video OCR, public code, credentials, and future research questions. They can navigate by slide controls, keyboard, URL hash, or touch and contact the author by email, GitHub, or LinkedIn.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The public site is a self-contained GitHub Pages portfolio at `yixin.blog`.
- Private raw materials must never be published to GitHub. The remote repository must remain a clean GitHub Pages repository.
- Current public portfolio assets are documentary derivatives of a private canonical archive; source PDFs, project folders, and original video remain outside the repository.
- The site can link to public work and an opt-in hosted cabinet-operation video; it is not a data product, research archive, or interactive application.

## Brand Commitments

- Identity: Yixin Zhang / YZ; robotics and applied AI.
- Voice: precise, research-oriented, evidence-aware, and candid about uncertainty.
- Preserve the distinction between public, inspectable evidence and broader claims that have not been independently established.
- State the current PhD search as an understated fact rather than a visitor-directed invitation.

## Evidence on Hand

- Public portfolio derivatives and their provenance are catalogued in `assets/portfolio/README.md`.
- The site contains project artifacts, selected metric tables, public code and pull-request links, credentials, contact routes, and a hosted cabinet-operation demonstration.
- `portfolio-evidence-plan.md` records the evidence boundaries for each case and should guide how existing claims are represented.

## Product Principles

1. Lead with research relevance and credible evidence rather than breadth alone.
2. Make each project inspectable: communicate the problem, the work, the supporting artifact, and the remaining uncertainty.
3. Do not turn incomplete evidence into stronger performance, ownership, reliability, or generalization claims.
4. Keep private source material private while maintaining a tidy, deployable public portfolio.
5. Make it simple for the right research reviewer to understand the trajectory and start a conversation.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The portfolio should remain usable without a mouse: preserve semantic structure, keyboard-first slide navigation, visible focus, direct controls, meaningful alternative text, reduced-motion support, and a readable responsive layout.
