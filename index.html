03 / 08 · Industrial computer vision

From floc images to operator advice

Two wastewater PoCs: expert-aligned dose adjustment from underwater video, and RGB oil-film early warning.

Retrospective record Metric conflict
Problem. Operators see floc morphology before downstream quality settles, but one frame is a noisy and incomplete view of a delayed chemical process.

Implemented PoC · best-supported scope

Five-class dose adjustment

  • Fixed-camera footage from Xidagou and Beidagou plant settings.
  • Binary masks and morphology: count, area ratio, equivalent diameter, fractal dimension, circularity.
  • XGBoost / LightGBM-style output: -20%, -10%, 0, +10%, or +20%.

Second PoC

Oil-film alert, not thickness estimation

An RGB-only early-warning concept for inspection and skimming. Segmentation, persistence rules, and alarm hysteresis belong to the production path; they are not proven deployment results here.

Model boundary

A pragmatic feature pipeline

ObserveUnderwater RGB video
SegmentFloc / water mask
Describe2D morphology features
ClassifyTree-based model
AdviseExpert-aligned class
2 Plant settings named in the retrospective
5 Discrete adjustment classes
89–92% Conflicting offline accuracy reports

Review before publication as a result

The hardest part is temporal, not visual

The résumé says 89%; retrospective notes say about 92%. Original splits and logs are absent. The 2–4h response delay, exact-dose regression, sensor fusion, digital twins, and offline RL are follow-on work—not implemented outcomes.

Method boundary

2D floc features are correlated indicators of process state. They are not physical measurements of true 3D floc structure, settling velocity, or a universally optimal PAC dose.
